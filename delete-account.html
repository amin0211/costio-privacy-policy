Costio Account and Data Deletion Request If you want to delete your Costio account and associated data, please contact us by email: Email: costio@costio.com Please use the subject line: Delete my Costio account In your email, include the email address used for your Costio account. When we receive your request, we will delete your account and associated app data, including: - Profile information - Expense records - Categories - Workspace data - Member data - Budget data - Income and account records Some data may be kept temporarily if required for security, fraud prevention, legal, or backup purposes. Backup copies may remain for up to 90 days before being permanently deleted. You can also request deletion of some app data without deleting your full account by contacting us at the same email address.
